## 2. Market Insights Summary

Our 5 specialized ASO subagents analyzed the Indian retro gaming market across five key lanes:

- **Cultural & Linguistic Context**: Retro gaming in India is heavily driven by Hinglish (blended Hindi-English) and nostalgia. The term **"bachpan wale game"** (childhood games) and **"purane game"** (old games) are the primary emotional search triggers. Consoles are historically called **"video game"** or **"tv wale game"**, cartridges are called **"cassette"** or **"kaset"**, and controllers are called **"remote"**.
- **Store Autocomplete Suggestions**: Store searches frequently target tutorials and games in local languages: `mario game kaise download kare`, `pokemon game gba hindi`, `ppsspp settings lag fix hindi`, and `purane video game download android`.
- **Bilingual Search Habits**: Users mix English terms with Hindi verbs/prepositions (e.g., *kaise download kare*, *mobile me kaise khele*, *bina internet ke game* for offline games, *kam mb ke game* for low file size games, *sabse best* for the absolute best).
- **Console & IP Brand Map**:
  - *Safe local concepts*: *bachpan wale game, cassette wale game, remote wale game, purane video game, dukaan wale game, parlor wale game*.
  - *Clone brands (Caution/Review)*: Mitashi (Game-In Champ), Terminator 2 (Ending Man), Samurai Electronics, Media TV Game, Little Master.
  - *Blacklist candidates (Consoles & Game IPs)*: Trademarked brands like *Nintendo, Game Boy, Sega, PlayStation, Mario, Pokémon, Zelda, Contra, Tekken 3, Cadillacs and Dinosaurs* (locally known as **"Mustapha game"**).
- **Feature-Based Keyword Expansion**: Core features mapped to Hinglish terms: `ek emulator me saare game` (multi-console), `game save kaise kare` (save state), `speed kaise badhaye` (fast forward), and `remote se game kaise khele` (gamepad mapping).

## 6. Policy And Filtering Notes

- **Famiclone Brands & Dead Consoles**: Clone hardware names (*Mitashi*, *Terminator 2*, *Samurai*, *Little Master*) are unlicensed entities that are no longer actively traded. While they carry minor trademark risks, they can be treated as caution/review terms in the downstream pipeline.
- **Copyrighted Game IPs**: Iconic game names (*Mario*, *Contra*, *Mustapha*, *Tekken 3*, *Pokémon*) are heavily protected trademarks. They must be completely blacklisted from metadata implementation (App Title, Short Description, Full Description) to prevent app suspensions.
- **ROM/BIOS Disclaimer**: The long description must explicitly declare that the application does not include any pre-loaded games or BIOS files, and is purely a runner for user-provided files.

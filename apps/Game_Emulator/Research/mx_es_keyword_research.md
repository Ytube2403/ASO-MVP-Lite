## 2. Market Insights Summary

Our 5 specialized ASO subagents analyzed the Mexican retro gaming market across five key lanes:

- **Cultural & Linguistic Context**: Retro gaming in Mexico is heavily defined by the neighborhood arcade culture of the 80s/90s. The term **"maquinitas"** is the absolute dominant local slang for arcade cabinets, which were commonly hosted in pharmacies (*farmacias*) and corner stores (*tienditas*). Challenging other players is referred to as **"retas"** (or *echar la reta*), and game tokens are called **"fichas"**. 
- **Store Autocomplete Suggestions**: Local search suggestions on the Play Store focus heavily on free access, offline play, and local slang: `emulador de maquinitas de farmacia`, `juegos retro sin internet`, `emulador gba gratis`, and `juegos gba en español`.
- **Bilingual Search Habits**: Mexican gamers mix English technical terms (*GBA, emulator, ROMs, save state, fast forward, cheats*) with Spanish grammar (e.g., *como jugar gba en celular*, *descargar emulador gba para android*, *emulador gba sin lag*).
- **Console & IP Brand Map**: 
  - *Safe local concepts*: *maquinitas, chispas, juegos de la infancia, juegos viejos*.
  - *Clone brands (Caution)*: *Polystation* (Famiclone shaped like PS1), *Family Game*, *creation, alien*.
  - *Blacklist candidates (Consoles & Game IPs)*: Trademarked brands like *Nintendo, Game Boy, Sega, PlayStation, Mario, Pokémon, Zelda, Metal Slug, Street Fighter, The King of Fighters (KOF)*. KOF is highly nostalgic in Mexico ("retas de KOF").
- **Feature-Based Keyword Expansion**: Core product features mapped to Mexican Spanish terms: `emulador todo en uno` (multi-console), `guardar partida` / `cargar partida` (save/load state), `controlador bluetooth` (gamepad mapping), `acelerar juego` (fast forward), and `emulador sin lag` / `emulador para gama baja` (performance optimization).

## 6. Policy And Filtering Notes

- **Trademark Protection**: All original console brands (e.g., *Nintendo, Sega, PlayStation, Game Boy*) and game IPs (e.g., *Mario, Pokémon, KOF, Metal Slug*) must remain strictly research-only and blacklisted from the final metadata slots to prevent trademark violations and potential app suspension.
- **Clone Brands**: Terms like `polystation` and `family game` carry minor compliance risks. They are highly relevant to Mexican childhood nostalgia but should be treated with caution.
- **Illegal Gambling vs. Arcades**: Ensure that all marketing copy distinguishes "maquinitas de videojuegos" (safe, video games) from "maquinitas tragamonedas/azar" (risky, gambling) to avoid triggering Google Play's gambling policy filters.
- **ROM/BIOS Disclaimer**: Standard developer guidelines require emulators to explicitly state in the description that no games/ROMs are included in the package, and that the app is simply a utility to run legally acquired backups.

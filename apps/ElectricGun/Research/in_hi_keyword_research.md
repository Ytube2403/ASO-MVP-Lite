## 1. App Positioning And Competitor Summary

- **App Positioning:** The app simulates an electric stun gun taser using visual sparks, camera LED strobe flash, strong haptic vibration, and realistic electrical buzzing sounds. It also features a haircut/clipper prank sound mode.
- **Indian Market Search Insights:** 
  - Indian users search heavily in both **Devanagari script** and **Hinglish** (Hindi words spelled phonetically in the Latin alphabet).
  - The concept of "electric shock" translates to **`bijli ka jhatka`** (phonetic) or **`बिजली का झटका`** (Devanagari).
  - The stun gun is colloquially searched as **`current wali bandook`** (shock-giving gun) or **`behosh karne wali gun`** (incapacitating/stun gun).
  - Secondary haircut/razor pranks are searched as **`baal katne wala prank`** (hair cutting prank) or **`baal katne wali machine`** (hair clipper).

## 1. App Positioning And Competitor Summary

- **App Positioning:** The app simulates an electric stun gun taser using visual sparks, camera LED strobe flash, strong haptic vibration, and realistic electrical buzzing sounds. It also features a haircut/clipper prank sound mode.
- **Mexico/Spanish Search Insights:** 
  - Standard translations like **`pistola de descargas`** (discharge gun) and **`choque eléctrico`** (electric shock) are frequently used.
  - The term **`taser`** (sometimes written with an accent as **`táser`**) is the most common industry and search term.
  - Hair clipper pranks are translated as **`máquina de cortar cabello`**, **`rasuradora`**, or **`cortadora de pelo`**.
  - Social terms like **`trollear amigos`** or **`bromas para amigos`** (pranks for friends) have very high search relevance.

## 1. App Positioning And Competitor Summary

- **App Positioning:** The app simulates an electric stun gun taser using visual sparks, camera LED strobe flash, strong haptic vibration, and realistic electrical buzzing sounds. It also features a haircut/clipper prank sound mode.
- **Indonesian Market Insights:** 
  - The word **`setrum`** (or verb form **`nyetrum`**) is the most common colloquial and natural term in Indonesia for electric shocks, tasers, and stun guns. Users search for `setrum` and `kejut listrik` much more than the English word `taser`.
  - There is a strong semantic crossover in Indonesia with **`vape n pod trik simulator`** (vape simulator apps), as many Indonesian prank apps bundle vape tricks, time bombs, gun sounds, and taser shocks into the same simulation package.
  - Secondary prank modes like hair clippers (**`alat cukur`**) are highly searched as joke elements.

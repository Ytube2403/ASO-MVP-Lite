## 1. App Positioning And Competitor Summary

- **App Positioning:** The app simulates an electric stun gun taser using visual sparks, camera LED strobe flash, strong haptic vibration, and realistic electrical buzzing sounds. It also features a haircut/clipper prank sound mode.
- **Vietnamese Market Insights:** 
  - The terms **`súng điện`** (stun gun), **`chích điện`** (electrocuting), and **`roi điện`** (stun baton) are the primary search phrases used by local users.
  - In Vietnam, users frequently look for terms combining these with **`giả lập`** (simulator) or **`troll bạn bè`** / **`trêu bạn bè`** (troll/prank friends).
  - Secondary modes like hair clipper/haircut pranks are commonly searched as **`tông đơ cắt tóc`** or **`cắt tóc troll`**.

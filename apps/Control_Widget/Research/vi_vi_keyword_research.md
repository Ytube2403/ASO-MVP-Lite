## 1. App Positioning And Competitor Summary

Control Widget is a personalization/widget app for Android users who want a custom quick panel/control panel with aesthetic themes, wallpapers/icons/widgets matching, and practical shortcuts such as brightness, volume, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, flashlight, screenshot, screen recording, airplane mode, and Do Not Disturb. It is not a launcher replacement, not a real iOS system app, and not a full widget-builder like KWGT.

Competitor and market sources scanned:

- Own app profile and Google Play listing for `Control Widget: Theme & Panels`.
- Direct control-panel competitors: `Control Widget - Themed Panels`, `Control Center - On Dinh & De`, `Control Widget & Themed Panels`, `Quick Control Widgets`, `iControl Widgets: Themed Panel`.
- Adjacent personalization competitors: `KWGT Kustom Widget Maker`, `Themix: Theme, Widget, Control`.
- Vietnamese Android terminology references around `Cai dat Nhanh`, `bang cai dat nhanh`, `trung tam dieu khien`, `thanh thong bao`, and iOS-style control center queries.

## 6. Policy And Filtering Notes

- Keep `ios`, `iphone`, `apple`, `dynamic island`, and `assistive touch` as research-only/platform-style audit terms. They indicate demand but should be handled by the later risk policy.
- Keep competitor brands such as `mi control center`, `power shade`, `one shade`, `kwgt`, `themix`, and `widgy` for blacklist and competitor mapping.
- Safe seed strategy should favor generic Vietnamese and bilingual category terms: `bang dieu khien`, `cai dat nhanh`, `control panel android`, `quick settings android`, `widget dieu khien`, `theme bang dieu khien`.
- Feature seed strategy should stay close to actual app functionality: theme library, quick panel customization, brightness/volume/Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/flashlight/screenshot/screen recording shortcuts, and home-screen visual matching.
- Do not promote unsupported behavior such as replacing the Android system panel at OS level, bypassing permissions, or promising every shortcut works on every device.
